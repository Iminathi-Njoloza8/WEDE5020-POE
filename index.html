ECOFRESH GROCERS

EcoFresh Grocers (Pty) Ltd is a fictional South African company established in 2022 in Eastern Cape, King Williams Town. The company was founded to provide customers with affordable, fresh, and locally sourced fruits, vegetables, and household grocery products. Since opening, the company has been known for supporting local farmers while offering quality products and excellent customer service. The company now wishes to expand its online presence by launching a modern website where customers can browse products and place orders online.
