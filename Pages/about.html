About EcoFresh Grocers

EcoFresh Grocers is a fictional South African grocery business established in 2022 in King William's Town, Eastern Cape. The business concept focuses on making fresh fruit, vegetables and household groceries easier to access.

Our website is designed to help customers learn about the business, explore a sample product range and ask questions before making a purchase. We also recognise the value of supporting local farmers and suppliers.

This website is an academic project. Product listings, prices and forms are examples for demonstration and should be confirmed before a real transaction.
