## Project Overview
I have created a website for a fictional company that mainly focuses on selling fruits and vegetables.
This company is called EcoFresh Grocers, and it was established in 2022 in Eastern Cape, King Williams Town. The company is known for supporting local farmers while offering quality products and excellent customer service.

## Website Goals and Objectives
  - Allow customers to order online.
  - Promote weekly specials and seasonal products.
  - Improve customer satisfaction.
  - Increase monthly sales by 40%.

## Key Features and Functionality
  - Fresh grocery products
  - Locally sourced products
  - Home delivery
  - Weekly specials
  - Customer accounts
  - Secure online payments
  - Customer support
  - Product search and categories
